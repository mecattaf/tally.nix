# Tally after zeta — the final state, and what stands on it

*Written 2026-08-14, the evening the run armed. This is the portrait of the
system as it exists when the zeta chapter gate goes green on Aug 16 — what is
then true, what is then deleted, what remains, and what it carries:
agency next, sodimo-final in parallel.*

## What tally is, after zeta

Two planes, one repository, one enforcement ladder.

The **work plane** is what three weeks built: a campaign is durable desired
state — a committed worklist DAG on the remote base — reconciled by short
stateless passes. One lane is one agent in one worktree producing one
witnessed commit; gates are the only merge criterion; receipts record every
actor; main advances only through a campaign merge; release proves through
the exact oracle it names. After ext0's boundary deploy, the ceremony is
structurally gone rather than suppressed: budgets are epoch-scoped and
refresh by derivation, a refusal names its paths in one typed envelope, a
wrong retry never dispatches because the judge said blocked, and the squash
layer adopts the lane's own words.

The **authority plane** is what zeta added: above the worklist, never
replacing it, sits the spec — a claim registry, not prose. Every claim is one
arrow line, `condition → observable`, with exactly one oracle binding and a
provenance mark that decides which direction is authoritative. DECIDE claims
grade the code through the gate ladder; BELIEVE claims are the author's model
of the tree, and the tree falsifies them — a renamed symbol under a BELIEVE
line fails the next gated head the day the drift starts. Doubt has syntax and
it blocks: an unsourced numeral, an invented identifier, an open DECISION
line at ratification are build failures, not review comments. The two planes
join through `trace.json`, append-only: claim to task at the sitting, claim
to merged commit and witness ref at release. "Which spec graded this code" is
a lookup, not a recollection.

The joint is mechanical. `crates/spec-lint` — one Rust parser, three
surfacings — runs as a flake check on every fleet-gated head, and the check
proves its own bite inside the same derivation by replaying a must-fail
corpus against an exact defect map. The grind's hardest lesson is now a
standing law with an enforcer: a bar without a gate is not a bar, and this
bar executed on its first day and cannot silently stop.

## A campaign's life, end to end

Authoring is a conversation with a linter, not a discipline held in the head.
The author — Claude Code, the only trusted spec author — reads the real
tree, writes claims, marks what it read BELIEVE and what it ruled DECIDE,
and loops against `spec-lint` until blocking defects are zero. The falsity
pass — "which of these statements about my codebase are false?" — is the one
human-shaped review that matters, and the human answers typed questions,
never edits a line. Ratification is an ordinary operator commit: steer from
anywhere, ratify at a keyboard.

The sitting compiles spec to worklist: census against the observed tree,
doubt drained, stage authored with goals that cite claim ids instead of
restating them and readFirst anchors that resolve or fail the lint, trace
rows appended, one commit. Then the factory runs unattended: lanes pull,
gates witness, the judge diagnoses, escalations arrive typed. At the close,
the campaign's own tool renders the coverage table — claim, task, acceptance,
evidence, merged sha — and release proves through it. The operator's total
for a campaign of epsilon's size: on the order of sixteen acts, every one at
a boundary, none of them transcription.

## What got deleted

The zeta ledger, by the placement law: the manual analyze pass; checking
pointers by eye (the 48-phantom-pointer class, closed); the hand-maintained
trace table; the hand-rendered close-out table; the read-every-number
confirmation sweep; the "is this requirement tested anywhere" judgment
(coverage is an enumeration now); the pre-derivation hedge re-read; the
anti-rot audit glance; the day-doc sprawl and scratchpad ledgers as the
system's memory — the record now lives where citations resolve, under
`evidence/`, at the authority revision. The supervisor role itself is on the
same ledger: the arm that ran this program is the run that retires it.

## What honestly remains

Zeta lands between ext0 and ext1, deliberately. The last mile is still the
epsilon-extension program's to build: the scoped deny-list that mechanizes
what tonight is authoring discipline; poll re-admission, after which a push
is the arming act; the lease; the re-gated auto-publish; the inbox that
makes the typed-doubt queue a delivery surface; and ext2's unified completion
contract, where release renders coverage from durable facts by itself. What
zeta changed is not whether that mile gets built but how it gets authored:
ext1 is the first stage ever derived per `skills/author-spec`, with real
anchors, against a committed spec — the two-way loop flying on its first
production input. The judge-tier corpus replay remains runnable-but-unrun,
and the model catalog stays where the local-models detour argued it belongs:
occupancy is a host fact, slots are defined by measured adequacy, and no
model name ever touches authority bytes.

## The backbone for agency

Agency is why the format was biased the way it was. Twenty frozen spec-kit
domains, an 823-line constitution, byte-level contracts, zero implementation
cadence — waiting, per FRONT-12, on exactly this: a released, documented
tally with a spec layer that can carry a domain through derivation into
campaigns without translation. That thaw condition is now concrete. The
recast of an agency domain into house grammar is mechanical in shape —
requirements become claim groups, contracts move under `contracts/` with
double-pinned schemas, the friction-log's byte-oracle-or-nothing is already
the L9 census — and the properties agency needs most are the ones the format
was elicited for. G1, flag-off-behaves-stock, is one giant Unchanged section
graded continuously. The Igalia delta discipline is a gate argv on
`git diff --shortstat`. The dialect bridge — the first contract of
consequence — gets the grind, with the lint as its standing bar. And the
scale argument holds by construction: a spec is a delta-of-intent against a
substrate, and tally's three weeks and Chromium's thirty million lines are
the same problem at different magnitudes, which is precisely why nothing in
the grammar names tally.

The honest caveat travels too: the format is proven at tally's scale on Aug
16, not at agency's. The first agency sitting should be treated as a pilot
in the D13 sense — record friction, don't absorb it — and the format amended
by ratification where the load finds seams.

## Sodimo-final, and the closing loop

Sodimo-final is the proving load: the campaign that demonstrates tally on
work that isn't tally. Its handoff is what the authority plane was shaped
for — a deliverable whose spec, trace, and coverage table are committed
bytes a receiving team can read without access to any conversation. The
close-out is a rendered join, the evidence resolves at the revision, and
"done" is a release receipt, not an assurance. If the epsilon chapters
proved the factory can build itself, sodimo-final proves the factory ships
to someone else — and closing that loop is what makes the agency bet a
sequencing decision instead of a leap.

## The keystone, restated once

Anthropic ships spec-driven development as deliberately unfinished
primitives: Plan Mode plans and discards, CLAUDE.md instructs without
enforcement, orchestration trusts blindly. Tally after zeta is those
primitives completed — the plan made durable and ratifiable, the instruction
made lintable, the trust made certifiable by receipts, the feedback loop
made typed. The move is the same one that built flows from ultracode:
harden what the tool already wants to do into what the factory can prove.
That is the backbone agency gets to stand on — not a bigger prompt, but a
system in which being wrong is a build failure, in either direction, the day
it happens.


## Appendix — the v0.0.1 blessing


One act deliberately sits outside the zeta program and outside every
campaign: the minting of the first real release. The version tally carries
today is an artifact of its own construction — the lineage that built the
factory, not the factory's product — and the Aug 3 ruling already named the
cure. The v0.0.1 goal is achieved by resetting *history*, not by rewriting
*code*: the history reset and the cleanup are separable, and conflating them
buys risk with no return, because a rewrite pass discards exactly the
invisible correctness that does not look load-bearing — the fixes the first
system silently got right.

The shape, as ruled then and still standing: one planned cleanup run, three
lanes, most of it cheap-tier — the comment sweep, the shim-and-vestige
excision with the fleet gate as safety net, and the one genuinely
architectural seam ruling, which is an operator decision, not a lane's
choice. Then the cleaned tree is cut over as **v0.0.1's root commit**: a
fresh history clear of issue numbering and PR lineage, the epoch where the
public record begins. Every `#NNN` referent in the old record becomes a
dangling pointer at that moment, which is one more reason the authority
plane's evidence directories matter — the citations that survive the cut are
the ones committed under `specs/**`, resolvable at a revision, not the ones
pointing into a forge history that will no longer exist.

Two conditions guard the mint, and both are ratchets, not dates. First, the
Aug 10 question — *can tally carry its own development* — must stay answered
under load: not one good session but a proven daily driver, with agency
domains and sodimo-final flowing through it as ordinary campaigns. Second,
the blessing itself is reflexive by design: **v0.0.1 is minted by tally, with
tally** — the cleanup run is a campaign, the cut-over is witnessed, and the
first release on the fresh history proves through `tally campaign release`
against its own coverage. The factory's first public version is its own
receipt. Until both ratchets hold, the current numbering stays what it is —
scaffolding, wearing its wrongness honestly — because a version blessed
before the daily-driver proof would be the one artifact in the system that
claims more than its evidence.
