## Method and coverage

- **Verified** means the shipped implementation agrees and a focused automated assertion pins the
  behavior. Each verified row cites implementation and check evidence.
- **Divergent** means the quoted doctrine and shipped behavior differ. Each divergent row cites the
  doctrine line in the claim column and the contrary implementation, plus a focused check where one
  exists, in the evidence column.
- **True-but-untested** means source inspection agrees, but no focused automated assertion was
  found. A schema, comment, downstream test, or test of only part of a compound claim is not promoted
  to verified.
- Independently falsifiable clauses are separate rows. Immediate restatements are covered by the
  same row and cite every relevant doctrine range; a closed field or trigger enumeration is one
  claim when it is validated as one contract.
- Headings, code-fence syntax, sample-specific names/paths/literals, recommendations, authoring
  advice, security rationale, hypothetical counterfactuals, and historical provenance are not
  behavioral claims. The dated 2026-08-10 timings at lines 1026-1030 and the historical issue/commit
  references at lines 1048 and 1180 are therefore not verdict rows. Current behavioral consequences
  stated around them are rows below.
- GitHub notification behavior is included even though the repository cannot assert the third-party
  platform result. The doctrine's exact claim is consequently true-but-untested rather than silently
  omitted.
