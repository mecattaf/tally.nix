## Method and coverage

- **Verified** means the shipped implementation agrees and a focused automated assertion pins
  the behavior. Each verified row cites implementation and check evidence.
- **Divergent** means the quoted doctrine and shipped behavior differ. Each divergent row cites
  the doctrine phrase and the contrary implementation or check.
- **True-but-untested** means source inspection agrees with the doctrine, but no focused automated
  assertion was found. Schema declarations, implementation comments, and tests of only a
  downstream consequence are not promoted to verified.
- Independently falsifiable clauses are split. Immediate restatements are covered by one row; a
  closed field/state enumeration enforced as one contract is one claim.
- Headings, navigation, Markdown/console-fence syntax, illustrative issue URLs and command
  literals, operator recommendations, support-status characterizations, historical
  incident/version provenance, security rationale, and hypothetical counterfactuals are not
  behavioral claims. Their asserted shipped consequences
  are included. In particular, the explanations for digest authority (`campaigns.md:403-404`),
  publication ordering (`campaigns.md:555-558`), the 2026 Codex probe (`campaigns.md:2134-2138`),
  host-tuning placement (`campaigns.md:2177-2192`), and the manual recovery advice/command
  (`campaigns.md:2240-2243,2257-2263`) are excluded as rationale or advice; the corresponding
  authorization, ordering, classification, tuning, cleanup, and re-entry behavior is audited.
- The first boundary stops before `## Campaigns on a NixOS host`; the second stops before
  `## Starting recurring automation`. Nothing from either following section is included.
