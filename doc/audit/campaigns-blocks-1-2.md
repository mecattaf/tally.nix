## Method and coverage

- **Verified** means the shipped implementation agrees and an automated assertion pins the
  behavior. Each such row cites both implementation and check evidence.
- **Divergent** means the quoted doctrine and shipped behavior differ. Each such row cites the
  doctrine line in the claim column and the contrary implementation/check lines in the evidence
  column.
- **True-but-untested** means the implementation agrees, but no focused automated assertion was
  found. These are hardening candidates; source inspection alone is not promoted to “verified.”
- Claims are split at independently falsifiable clauses. A repeated restatement is covered in one
  row with all relevant doctrine lines. A closed field/default/error enumeration governed by one
  decoder branch is one schema claim, not one row per member. Explanatory rationale is not
  duplicated when its behavioral consequence already has a row.
- Headings, navigation links, authoring advice, placeholder issue numbers, and sample-specific
  literals are not behavioral claims. The JSON and console examples at lines 72–162 are therefore
  not asserted to describe a real `/srv` checkout or issues `#42/#43`; every schema- or
  command-bearing behavior they illustrate is covered below. The Markdown example at lines
  241–245 *does* assert a generated format and has its own row.
- The sentence beginning at line 287 is included through its end at line 291 because claim text
  intersects the last in-scope line. No claim beginning after line 288 is included.
