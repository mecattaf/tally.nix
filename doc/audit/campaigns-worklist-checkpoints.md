# Campaigns doctrine audit: worklists and checkpoints

This is the modality record for forge task `doc-oracle-worklist-checkpoints` (#515). It audits
every falsifiable behavioral claim from `## The recurring worklist node contract` through
`### What binds a forge-native checkpoint receipt` in `doc/src/flows/campaigns.md:1321-1676`
at repository revision `cf295da4e3ca4f27388ba108002bba79630d0a84`. It deliberately does
not reconcile the doctrine prose or add checks.

## Method and coverage

- **Verified** means the shipped implementation agrees and a focused automated assertion pins
  the behavior. Each verified row cites both implementation and check evidence.
- **Divergent** means the quoted doctrine and shipped behavior differ. Each divergent row cites
  the doctrine phrase and the contrary implementation or check.
- **True-but-untested** means source inspection agrees with the doctrine, but no focused automated
  assertion was found. Schema declarations, implementation comments, and tests of only a
  downstream consequence are not promoted to verified.
- Claims are split at independently falsifiable clauses. An immediate restatement is covered by
  the same row; a closed field or state enumeration is one claim when it is enforced as one
  schema.
- Headings, JSON-fence syntax and illustrative sample literals, recommendations, authoring advice,
  security rationale, historical provenance, and hypothetical counterfactuals are not behavioral
  claims. Their asserted shipped consequences are audited. In particular, the trust discussion at
  lines 1469-1474, ruleset/credential advice at lines 1603-1610, legacy-tag cleanup commands at
  lines 1595-1600, and decommissioning advice at lines 1673-1675 are excluded as such; the actual
  execution, namespace, compatibility, failure, and retention behaviors around them are included.
- The boundary is the blank line before `## Reconciliation, parallelism, and the merge criterion`
  at line 1678. Nothing beginning in that next section is included.
